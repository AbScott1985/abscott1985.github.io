Attendance tracking

A gymnastics attendance app built for how clubs really work

Paper registers are slow, unreliable and create unnecessary admin work. Apps By Angela replaces them with a digital gymnastics register that works on any phone or tablet — taking seconds rather than minutes, and saving automatically so nothing is ever lost.

Coaches see their classes on their homepage the moment they open the app. Every register shows each child's name alongside any health conditions, medications, allergies and photo permissions — all the information a coach needs, right there before class begins. Coaches can also open a gymnast's skill cards and badges directly from the register screen, so there is no need to go hunting through separate menus during class.

Notes, progress and recognition all sit close to the register, helping coaches keep attendance, development and achievements connected in the same workflow.

Whether you have one class or ten running simultaneously, the Apps By Angela gymnastics attendance app handles it all — including merged registers for classes that share training time, making it even faster to manage busy club schedules.

The feature everyone loves

Kids sign themselves in by tapping their colour tile

Place a tablet or iPad at the entrance to your gym and let the gymnasts sign themselves in. Every child has their own unique colour tile — they simply tap it as they walk in and they're on the register instantly.

It's perfect for younger children who can't yet read — they recognise their colour and tap it without any help needed. It's fast, fun, and completely removes the need for a coach to stand at the door calling out names.

Coaches can still add late arrivals manually with a single tap — so whether children self sign-in or the coach manages the register, it all ends up in the same place, saved automatically.

All attendance features

Everything your gymnastics club registers need

📋
Coach Register on Phone or Tablet

Coaches see all their classes directly on their homepage. They open a class, see the full list of members enrolled, and mark attendance with a single tap. Works on any iPhone, Android phone or iPad — no special hardware needed.

🎨
Self Sign-In by Colour Tile

Set up a tablet at the gym entrance and let gymnasts sign themselves in by tapping their unique colour tile. Ideal for younger children who recognise their colour instantly. Fast, fun, and completely hands-free for coaches at the start of class.

👶
Add Unregistered Members to Register

If a new child arrives who hasn't registered on the app yet, coaches can add them to the register manually by entering their name and date of birth. When their parent later downloads the app and registers, the system automatically links them to that child — so their training history is never lost from day one.

⏱️
Training Hours Tracking

Every attendance is recorded and stored, allowing clubs to track exactly how many training hours each gymnast has completed per week and per month. Invaluable for clubs monitoring development, managing competition eligibility or simply keeping accurate records for safeguarding purposes.

🏥
Health Info Visible on Every Register

Every register displays each child's health conditions, medications, allergies and photo permissions alongside their name. Coaches have instant access to the information they need before every session — no need to hunt through paper files or ask parents to repeat themselves every week.

📝
Coach Notes on Register

Coaches can add notes directly on the register — visible to other coaches and administrators. Whether it's a note about an injury, a behavioural observation or a reminder for the next session, it's all stored in one place and accessible to everyone who needs it.

🏅
Skill Cards & Badges From the Register

Coaches can open a gymnast's skill cards, badges and progress straight from the register screen. No hunting through profiles or separate admin areas — the tools coaches need during class are right where they are already working.

🔀
Merged Registers for Simultaneous Classes

When multiple classes train at the same time — for example Sapphire squad running Tumble, Fig and Regional simultaneously — their registers can be merged into one. Coaches manage a single combined register instead of switching between separate ones, saving time and reducing errors.

💾
Registers Save Automatically

Every register is saved automatically as soon as attendance is marked. There's nothing to submit, nothing to export and nothing that can be accidentally lost. Admins can go back and view the register for any class on any date at any time.

📊
Full Register History for Admins

Administrators have full access to register history across all classes and locations. View who attended any session, check training hours for individual gymnasts, and keep accurate records — all from the web admin panel without needing to ask coaches for information.

From paper chaos to a digital register in seconds — coaches spend less time on admin and more time coaching.

Why it matters

Registers that protect your club

Accurate attendance records aren't just about knowing who's in the gym — they're a safeguarding requirement. In the event of a fire, evacuation or incident, coaches need to know instantly who is on site. Apps By Angela gives them that information in seconds.

Having health conditions, medications and allergies visible on every register means coaches are never caught off guard. And because everything saves automatically, your club always has a complete, accurate attendance history — no gaps, no lost paper registers, no guesswork.
