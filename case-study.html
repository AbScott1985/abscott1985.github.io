Forest of Dean Gymnastics Club

More Than Just a Membership App

How Apps by Angela is transforming the way we run Forest of Dean Gymnastics Club

An independent gymnastics club’s experience of a bespoke club management system.

Enquire Now

Running a busy gymnastics club involves so much more than coaching.

Behind every class, competition, achievement and event is an enormous amount of administration. From memberships, fees and registers to waiting lists, awards, parent communications and organising events, there is always something that needs managing.

At Forest of Dean Gymnastics Club, we have hundreds of gymnasts across our preschool, recreational and competitive programmes, each with different needs and training arrangements.

We wanted a system that would bring everything together, reduce the workload on our office and coaching teams, and make life easier for our families.

Most importantly, we wanted software that could work around the way our club operates, rather than having to change everything we do to fit the software.

Apps by Angela has already been a game changer for us!

Membership and Automatic Fee Management

Managing fees across a gymnastics club can be complicated, particularly when gymnasts train different numbers of hours each week or attend several classes.

The app automatically calculates fees based on each gymnast's total weekly training hours, rather than simply the number of classes or days they attend.

There is also a manual override, giving the office flexibility when individual circumstances require it.

Monthly invoices are automatically generated and sent to parents, removing a huge amount of repetitive administration from the office.

Membership charges, family accounts, payments and other invoices can all be managed within the same system.

For a club with hundreds of members, having these processes automated makes an enormous difference.

Class Management and Changes

One of the features we particularly appreciate is how class changes can be managed through the app.

Parents can submit requests directly, but coaches can also recommend class changes themselves.

A coach can select the gymnast's new class, specify the date the change should take effect and choose whether the new class should replace their existing class or be added alongside it.

The parent receives the request through the app and, once approved, the gymnast is automatically moved to or added to the appropriate register on the agreed date.

This is particularly useful for gymnasts progressing through recreational classes or adding additional training sessions.

It also means that coaches, parents and the office are all working from the same information, without needing numerous emails or messages to arrange a straightforward class change.

Cancellation Requests and Leaving Dates

Even cancellations are managed through the app.

Parents can submit a cancellation request, which the office can review and approve.

The system can calculate any outstanding fees on a pro rata basis, depending on the gymnast's agreed leaving date.

Once approved, the gymnast is automatically removed from the class register on the correct date.

This avoids having to keep separate reminders or manually remove gymnasts when their notice period ends.

It's another example of how something that previously required several administrative steps can now be handled through one process.

Waiting Lists and Class Availability

Managing waiting lists is a significant part of running a gymnastics club, especially when classes are regularly full.

With Apps by Angela, parents can add their child and browse the available classes suitable for their age.

If a class is full, they can request to join the waiting list directly through the app, without needing to contact the office.

The club can review waiting list requests, monitor class availability and offer places as spaces become available.

Parents can then respond to offers through the app, helping to streamline the process from initial enquiry to joining a class.

The system also collects important information about medical conditions, disabilities and additional support needs, helping the club consider appropriate classes and staffing arrangements.

Rather than relying on separate enquiry forms, emails and spreadsheets, waiting list administration is brought together in one place.

For both new families and our office team, this makes the joining process much more straightforward.

“It’s changing the way we manage the club, how our teams work together and how we communicate with our families.”

Registers and Attendance

Coaches can access their class registers through the app and record attendance directly.

They can also access relevant gymnast information, helping them manage their sessions more effectively.

Parents can report absences through the app, reducing the number of telephone calls and emails received by the office.

Having class information, attendance and gymnast records connected means less duplication and more up-to-date information for everyone.

Gymnastics Awards and Achievements

This is one of our favourite features!

Our coaches can record individual skills as gymnasts achieve them, with parents receiving notifications celebrating their child's progress.

Once all the required skills for an award have been completed, the office can review the achievement and issue the relevant invoice directly through the app.

The system tracks the awards process, including which gymnasts have completed awards, which invoices have been issued, which certificates and badges need preparing and which awards have been handed out.

Once an award has been completed, a little digital badge also appears on the gymnast's profile.

This gives gymnasts and parents a lasting record of their achievements and makes it easier for families to see how their child is progressing.

From a club perspective, it brings together the coaching, invoicing and administrative sides of our awards programme.

Rather than relying on handwritten lists and separate records, we can follow the entire process through the app.

And for the gymnasts, it's lovely to see their hard work recognised!

Competition Invitations and Entries

Organising competition entries involves much more than simply asking gymnasts whether they want to compete.

There are invitations to send, responses to track, entry fees to collect and information to manage.

Through the app, competition invitations can be sent directly to the relevant gymnasts and their families.

Parents can accept their invitations, with invoices automatically generated and the club able to track entries and payments.

This removes the need to manage competition invitations across separate emails, spreadsheets and payment records.

For a club regularly entering gymnasts into competitions, having everything in one place is a huge help.

Holiday Activities, Trials and Bookings

Alongside our regular classes, we run holiday activities, gymnastics camps, trials and additional sessions.

The app allows us to create bookable activities with dates, times, age restrictions, capacity limits and payment options.

Bookings can be made through the app, while public booking links allow us to promote activities to families who aren't already members.

This means we can advertise sessions to the wider community without needing a separate booking system.

Booking confirmations, payment arrangements and participant information can all be handled within the same platform.

It's particularly useful when we're promoting activities at short notice and need to manage bookings efficiently.

Event Management and Ticketing

This is another feature that really demonstrates how Angela listens to the needs of a club.

Our Christmas gymnastics displays involve multiple performances, limited tickets per family and the challenge of keeping families together.

Rather than having to use an external ticketing website, Angela has developed an event management and ticketing system within our club app.

We can create different performance sessions, each with its own time and capacity.

Gymnasts can be allocated to sessions, with families kept together where needed.

Ticket purchases can be restricted to families allocated to a particular session, with a maximum number of tickets per family.

Once the initial allocation period has finished, remaining tickets can be released for other families to purchase on a first come, first served basis.

The system also provides ticket sales information and downloadable attendance and door lists, including the gymnasts associated with each family.

There are even options for setting deadlines for families to register their interest in taking part.

For clubs organising Christmas displays, competitions, showcases or other events, this type of functionality can save an incredible amount of administration.

And having it integrated into our existing app, rather than paying for or managing another platform, is a massive bonus.

Parent Communication and the Homepage Carousel

Keeping families informed is an important part of running any club.

The app provides a central place for parents to access information about their children's classes, fees, payments, achievements and activities.

Notifications help keep families updated without the office having to send individual messages.

One feature we particularly love is the homepage carousel.

We can display important announcements, reminders, promotions and club information directly within the app.

Even better, the carousel supports clickable links.

This means that if we're advertising a holiday workshop, reminding parents about British Gymnastics membership or promoting an event, families can click the announcement and go straight to the relevant information or booking page.

It's a fantastic way to make sure important messages are visible and easy to act upon.

“Angela takes the time to understand not just what we’re asking for, but why we need it and how it would work in practice.”

Gift Aid Management for Charitable Clubs

As a registered charity, this is an especially valuable feature for us.

Gift Aid is an important source of additional income for eligible charitable payments, but keeping accurate records and preparing claims can be time-consuming.

The app includes a Gift Aid option, allowing us to record and track the relevant declarations and information within our membership system.

It also provides reporting that can be easily transferred into the documentation required for HMRC Gift Aid submissions.

This makes it much easier to keep track of the information needed for claims, without maintaining a completely separate set of records.

For a charitable club, where every additional pound can make a difference, having Gift Aid functionality built directly into the system is fantastic.

It's also a great example of Angela understanding that clubs aren't all run in the same way. Some are commercial businesses, while others, like ours, operate as charities.

One System Connecting the Whole Club

What makes Apps by Angela so valuable isn't simply the number of features it offers.

It's how those features work together.

A coach can recommend a class change, a parent can approve it and the gymnast's register can automatically update on the agreed date.

A gymnast can achieve a skill, their parent can receive a notification, the completed award can be reviewed and invoiced, and the office can track the certificate and badge through to presentation.

A competition invitation can be sent, accepted, invoiced and tracked.

A parent can request a cancellation, the office can approve the leaving date and the system can calculate the relevant fees and remove the gymnast from the register automatically.

One action leads to the next, without the office having to manually manage every stage.

For our office team, this means less repetitive administration, fewer separate systems and the potential to save a considerable amount of time.

For our coaches, it means easier access to registers, skills tracking and class management.

For our parents, it means greater convenience, clearer communication and a much better picture of their child's gymnastics journey.

And for the club as a whole, it means being able to concentrate more of our time and energy on what really matters: our gymnasts.
