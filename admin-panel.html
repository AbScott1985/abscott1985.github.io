Member management

Every member at your fingertips

The members screen gives admins a complete view of every parent account and their associated children. Search by name, view parent contact details, send individual push notifications, reset passwords or delete accounts — all from one screen without needing a spreadsheet.

Members screen — parent accounts, linked children, search, notifications and password reset

Class management

Disciplines, levels and schedules — all in one place

Disciplines are the activity types your club runs. Within each discipline you can create levels, and within each level you set up individual class times with their own day, time, capacity and enrolment status. Changes reflect instantly across the app.

Manage capacity, waiting lists and requests

Each class shows enrolled versus available spaces. Admins can open or close requests per class, manage waiting lists, and add new class times at any time — without needing to contact a developer or reconfigure anything.

Fees & payments

See exactly who has paid and who hasn't

The fees dashboard shows every child's monthly fees, registration and insurance status — and a total of fees due, paid and outstanding for the selected month. Filter by location, search by child name and drill into individual payment history.

Why it matters

Stop managing your club in spreadsheets

Most gymnastics clubs are still running on a patchwork of spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and paper registers. It works — until it doesn't. Things slip, records go out of date, and admin teams spend hours every week chasing information that should already be in one place.

Apps By Angela replaces all of that with a single web admin panel. Members, fees, classes, registers, notifications and orders — everything in one place, always accurate, accessible from any browser at any time.
