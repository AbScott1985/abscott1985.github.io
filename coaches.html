Coach features

A gymnastics coach app built for the way coaches actually work

Coaches don't sit at desks. They're on the gym floor, spotting, coaching and keeping thirty children safe — which is why paper registers get lost, skill progress lives in someone's head, and the admin team spends Sunday nights typing it all up.

Apps By Angela gives every coach their own login inside your club's app. They see their classes for the day, mark attendance with a tap, tick off skills the moment a gymnast lands them, and record scores on the spot. Everything syncs instantly to the admin panel — no transcribing, no chasing, no lost paperwork.

It works for gymnastics clubs, dance schools, cheerleading clubs, martial arts clubs and any other activity organisation across the UK. Here's everything coaches can do.

What coaches can do

Every coach feature, explained

📋
Digital Class Registers

Coaches see their classes for the day and mark attendance with a single tap. Arrival times are recorded automatically and late arrivals are flagged — so the club always knows exactly who was in the gym, and when.

🚨
Medical & Allergy Alerts at a Glance

Colour-coded badges on the register show medical conditions, allergies, medication and special requirements for every child — plus photo permissions. Vital safeguarding information is in front of the coach at all times, not filed away in a folder.

📈
Skill Cards & Progress Tracking

Coaches tick off skills the moment a gymnast masters them — with the coach's name and date recorded against every skill. They can add notes and choose to share them with parents, so progress is captured while it happens, not remembered later.

🃏
Skill Cards Straight from the Register

A child's skill cards are one tap away from the register itself. Coaches can open a card mid-class to sign off a new skill, or add a child to a skill card on the spot — no switching screens, no losing their place.

⭐
Gymnast Scores from the Register

Coaches can score gymnasts directly on the register during class. Scores feed straight through to the club's gymnast score records, powering awards and recognition without any extra admin.

🔀
Combined Class Registers

Running two levels together in one session? Combined classes get a single register with everyone on it, so shared sessions are just as quick to manage as regular ones.

➕
Add Children on the Spot

A trialist turns up, or a child moves class mid-term? Coaches can add a child to the class and register in seconds, right from the gym floor — the admin panel updates instantly.

🕓
Full Register History

Every register is saved automatically. Coaches and admins can look back at any past session — who attended, who was late, who was absent — which is invaluable for safeguarding, funding evidence and attendance queries.

📝
Notes on Every Child

Coaches can keep notes against any child on the register — a one-off reminder for this week, or a recurring note that appears every month. The right information follows the child from session to session.

📆
Personal Coach Calendar

Every coach gets their own personal calendar alongside the club calendar. They can add and edit their own events, so club sessions, competitions and personal commitments all live in one view.

💳
Private Lesson Management

Coaches can add, view and edit their own private lessons in the app. Every booking is visible in the admin panel automatically, so the club always has the full picture without asking.
