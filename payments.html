Payment tracking

Payments should connect to the way your club actually runs

Chasing money is one of the biggest time drains for gymnastics clubs. Monthly fees, one-off charges, invoices, credits, session bookings and membership changes can all overlap, and that is exactly where spreadsheets start to fall apart.

Apps By Angela keeps those moving parts together. Admins can add charges, track invoices, apply credits, manage memberships and record cancellation dates clearly, so it is easier to see what should be paid and when billing should stop.

For bookable sessions, existing members can book in the app, while new families can use a public web link and pay as they go without being added as full members first. Their details are still saved, ready if they decide to join later.

All payment features

How the payment system works

📊
Clear Finance Dashboard

Admins can see the money picture from one place: paid, unpaid, invoices, credits, charges and outstanding balances. It gives the club a clearer view without jumping between spreadsheets, bank statements and message threads.

🧾
Invoices That Stay Linked to Members

Create and track invoices against the right member or family, so admins can see what has been issued, what has been paid and what still needs following up. It keeps payment admin tied to the actual club record.

➕
One-off Charges Without the Mess

Add charges for extras, sessions, adjustments or club-specific payments without losing them in notes. The charge is visible, trackable and connected to the family account, so admins know exactly what has been added and why.

↩
Credits Recorded Properly

Credits can be recorded clearly instead of being remembered manually. If a family has money carried forward or an adjustment needs applying, the club can keep that information visible and easier to manage.

📅
Memberships & Cancellation Dates

Memberships can be managed alongside cancellation dates, making it clearer when a gymnast is leaving and what month billing should stop. It gives admins a proper record instead of relying on memory or scattered messages.

🔗
Bookable & Pay-as-you-go Sessions

Existing members can book sessions in the app, while new families can book through a public web link without joining first. Their details are saved, so if they become members later, the club already has what it needs.

💬
Payment Reminder Messages

Admins can send payment reminders directly from the system, either to one parent or to families with outstanding payments. Messages appear inside the app history, so everything is easier to trace.

🏦
Card, Bank Transfer or Cash

Parents can pay in the way your club chooses. Stripe card payments are supported, and bank transfer or cash payments can be confirmed by admins so the record still stays tidy.
